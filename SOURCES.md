## 2026-08-13 usage audit

The plugin was measured against 711 real transcripts rather than against intent, and cut from 56 artifacts to 38. The discriminator that mattered was **"did it leave artifacts on disk"**, not "was it invoked": one-shot bootstrap skills like `setup` and `create-standards-checker` have no recent invocations by design, and `describe-pr` looked unused by slash count while having written 25 description files.

Cut for zero invocations and zero output: `claude-automation-recommender`, `claude-md`, `finishing-a-development-branch`, `issue-triage`, `prototype`, `receiving-code-review`, `requesting-code-review`, `security-patterns`, `to-issues`, `using-git-worktrees`, `verification-before-completion`, `zoom-out`, `/research-codebase`, `/review-pr`, `/validate-plan`, `codebase-locator`, `codebase-pattern-finder`, `code-simplifier`, `conversation-analyzer`.

Three findings drove the behavior changes, and they are the ones worth remembering:

1. `general-purpose` was dispatched 141 times, more than every wai agent combined. Every wai agent was too narrow to accept "investigate this and fix it". Fixed by ADR-0001 (freeform `wai-implementer`) and ADR-0002 (nudge hook).
2. The owner bypassed their own skills where the skill added a step rather than removing one: 95 raw `git worktree add`, 26 raw `git commit`, 95 `gh pr create` against 25 generated descriptions.
3. An outside skill beat a plugin one head-to-head: `rigorous-pr-review` ran 10 times, `/review-pr` zero. The winner had opinionated standards; the loser had orchestration. Standards absorbed into `code-reviewer`.

Re-run the measurement before the next strip. Invocation counts come from `"skill":"..."` and `"subagent_type":"..."` in `~/.claude/projects/**/*.jsonl`; disk artifacts are the better signal.
